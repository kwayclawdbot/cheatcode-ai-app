### The credit system (migration 0030)
**A credit = a slice of cost, priced AS IF THE CACHE WERE ALWAYS WARM** — input +
cache reads×0.1 + output×price-ratio. **Cache-write premium excluded; we absorb cold
starts** (our cache state, not the user's behaviour, and indefensible at a support desk).
**4,000 units = 1 credit**, calibrated against real questions: distribution
`1·1·1·1·1·1·1·2·2·3`. Rounds the user's way (undercharges ~10%, measured).

| | price | allowance | ceiling | Trade |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | — | 10 credits/day | none | **no** |
| Pro | $59/mo | 40 credits/day (~25 questions) | $10/mo | yes |
| VIP | $99/mo | 75 credits/day (~50 questions) | $20/mo | yes |
| Top-up | $9 / 100 credits *(agent's proposal)* | never expires | — | — |

All numbers in ONE file: `apps/api/src/lib/kai/plans.ts`.
Tables: `kai_credit_periods` (unique on user+period — idempotent grant enforced by
the DB, not app logic), `kai_credit_wallets`, `kai_credit_ledger`. **RLS on, zero
policies, service-role only — verified on both databases.**
**Enforcement is at the ROUTE, not `stream.ts`** — a credit is one question, not one
model call. Checked before, charged after. **A failed answer is never charged**
(proved for free by the outage). In-flight always finishes. If the credit system is
unreadable, the message goes through and the log shouts.
Grant credits spend before purchased ones — enforced in the DB.
Two defects found by running it: mid-day upgrade left the user on 10 credits AND on
the free plan's absent ceiling; four calls stored with no cost because the ledger held
`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001` and the rate table only knew `claude-haiku-4-5`.
