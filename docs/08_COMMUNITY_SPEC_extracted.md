NORTH STAR  Every useful conversation can become research, a marked chart, an alert, a plan, a position update, or a lesson.

1. Product vision
Cheat Code AI combines AI intelligence, real traders and investors, structured education, and live market decisions in one workspace. Community does not sit beside the trading product like a separate Discord server. It is attached to the same assets, setups, charts, alerts, positions, portfolios, and outcomes that Kai already understands.
The integration should preserve two kinds of value: the speed and lived experience of human conversation, and the verification, structure, personalization, and memory supplied by Kai.

6. Community intelligence pipeline
Community activity is a discovery and context signal. Kai independently verifies material claims before incorporating them into research or a setup. Popularity must never substitute for evidence.
Collect eligible public-room activity under disclosed community-intelligence terms.
Extract assets, levels, catalysts, questions, sentiment, and conflicting interpretations.
Weight signals for account age, verified expertise, disclosure quality, manipulation patterns, and source diversity.
Verify material claims against market data, filings, news, and other authoritative sources.
Label what came from the community and what Kai independently concluded.
Use the result to suggest research, explain disagreement, update monitoring, or create an educational response.
Allowed uses
Detect unusual attention
Surface commonly mentioned levels
Find news or catalysts for verification
Identify unanswered beginner questions
Discover conflicting theses
Prioritize independent research
Measure sentiment shifts
Improve contextual education
Prohibited uses
Treat sentiment as proof
Change a grade solely because a room is excited
Expose private messages
Reveal personal portfolio data without consent
Present a popularity ranking as investment quality
Train on deleted or restricted content outside the disclosed policy
7. Structured participation and feedback
Casual conversation remains easy. When a user posts an idea, Kai can optionally help make it decision-ready before publication.
FIELD
KAI FEEDBACK

Evidence

Kai presents an improved draft for approval. It never silently rewrites a member, claims endorsement, or converts a post into a trade without explicit action.
8. Trust and contributor identity
Use evidence-based profile context instead of points, streaks, leaderboards, or profit contests.
Role labels: verified identity, experienced trader, long-term investor, educator, moderator.
Contribution history: ideas posted, thesis updates, outcomes disclosed, and use of defined risk.
Community feedback: usefulness and clarity—not agreement or profitability alone.
Position disclosure: whether the contributor currently holds the discussed asset.
No “top trader” ranking based on screenshots, raw P/L, trade frequency, or position size.

10. Safety, moderation, and market integrity
Community claims remain visibly unverified until checked.
Kai labels community sentiment separately from its analysis.
Primary sources and market data take precedence over room consensus.
Detect and down-weight coordinated promotion, spam, copy-paste campaigns, and suspicious new-account clusters.
Require position disclosure for structured trade-idea posts where appropriate.
Prohibit or clearly label paid promotion, affiliate activity, and compensated discussion.
Maintain moderation audit history for edited or deleted market claims while respecting user-facing deletion rules.
Provide mute, block, report, slow mode, restricted posting, and room lockdown controls.
Show monitoring health and never guarantee alert delivery.
Keep financial action preview, consent, and server-authoritative execution states intact.

15. Acceptance criteria
A user can move from a community idea to verified research, a marked chart, and an alert without losing context.
Every community-derived insight is labeled, timestamped, and separated from Kai’s independent conclusion.
Kai can summarize a defined room and time range with links to the underlying eligible posts.
A natural-language alert displays structured logic and dependencies before activation.
Every position can trace back to its research, setup, annotations, discussion, alerts, and plan.
Private messages and portfolio data are not incorporated without explicit applicable consent.
Coordinated promotion and suspicious sentiment manipulation can be detected, down-weighted, and reviewed.
The experience contains no XP, streaks, badges, leaderboards, or rewards for financial activity.
Mobile rooms remain readable during fast Day Trade activity and support mute, filter, and catch-up controls.
Live-session replays preserve transcript, chart events, sources, setups, and educational context.
