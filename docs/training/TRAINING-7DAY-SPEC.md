# Zero to Trade Ready in 7 Days — the owner's curriculum spec (2026-09-07, verbatim intent)

THE PROMISE: not "become an expert trader in 7 days." In 7 days, a complete
beginner should understand the market, read a basic setup, build a
risk-controlled trade plan, practice it, and intelligently use Kai as a
trading copilot. Minimum viable trading competence, not memorization.
Marketing position: "From Zero to Trade Ready in About 5 Hours Over 7 Days."
Graduates land directly in the core CCAI subscription.

## THE UI ORGANIZATION (supersedes the mockup's flat lesson list)
Never show "27 lessons." Show DAY 3 OF 7. Seven day nodes, each expanding
into 3–6 tiny lesson nodes:
- DAY 1 Market Basics · DAY 2 Read the Chart · DAY 3 Find the Setup ·
  DAY 4 Build the Trade · DAY 5 Execute the Plan · DAY 6 Trade With Kai ·
  DAY 7 Get Trade Ready
After graduation the next path can appear (Level 2: Become Consistent; Day
Trading / Swing Trading Playbooks; Options; Advanced TA) — but Trader
Foundations ends in seven days and gets out of the user's way.

## THE FUNNEL SHAPE
Days 1–2 READ (market → candles → structure → trend → levels) ·
Days 3–4 ANALYZE (setup → thesis → entry → stop → target → risk) ·
Day 5 EXECUTE (orders → discipline → management) · Day 6 PRACTICE (paper
trading with Kai) · Day 7 PROVE (assessment → graduation → live Kai).
Times: D1 30m · D2 40m · D3 45m · D4 50m · D5 40m · D6 60m · D7 60m ≈ 5h25m.

## EVERY LESSON'S RHYTHM (the three-prong system as one product)
1. Learn (2–4 min interactive concept) → 2. See (2–6 min human explanation)
→ 3. Do (interactive activity) → 4. Ask Kai (Kai drills the concept)
→ 5. Apply (real/historical chart) → 6. Master (score updates).
Content schema per lesson: Hook → Concept (ONE idea only) → Visual → Human →
Interaction → Kai (learner explains reasoning) → Real Market → Mastery →
Profile Update → Next Lesson. A lesson is an interactive experience where
video is only one ingredient — never a video with questions attached.

## DAY 1 — Understand What the Hell You're Looking At (~30 min, gate 70%)
L1 What Is a Stock? (3–4m interactive): company, shares, buyers/sellers,
price, market cap, why price changes. No Wall Street history, no jargon.
L2 How a Trade Works (4m): buy→moves→sell; then ENTRY→STOP→TARGET on day
one — everything hangs from them. Entry $100 / Stop $98 / Target $106 →
show Risk $2 / Reward $6 = 3:1 visually. Establish the model, don't deep-teach.
L3 Reading Candles (5–7m human+interactive): open/high/low/close, green/red,
wick, body. EXPLICITLY DO NOT teach pattern names (hammer, doji, morning
star…) — Kai teaches formations later contextually.
Kai Challenge: 5 candles — "buyers or sellers controlled this candle?",
"which showed rejection?"
Gate: bullish/bearish candle, entry/stop/target, timeframe, vocabulary. 70%.

## DAY 2 — Read the Market (~40 min) — the most important day
Outcome: look at a chart and answer up, down, or sideways.
L1 Market Structure (6m): HH, HL, LH, LL only. HH+HL=uptrend, LH+LL=downtrend.
L2 Trend (5m human): "How I identify the trend in 10 seconds" — real charts,
not diagrams.
L3 Support & Resistance (7m): where did price repeatedly react? Only support
+ resistance initially. Interactive: tap where support is → Kai overlays the
correct zone.
Kai Practice: 5 charts — trend? tap support. tap resistance. Kai grades.
Day 2 Challenge: fresh chart, no hints — trend, support, resistance.
Gates: Trend ≥80% · Structure ≥75% · Levels ≥70%.

## DAY 3 — Understand Setups (~45 min)
L1 A Trade Needs a Thesis — CRITICAL. "Because it's going up" isn't a thesis.
THE TRADE-PLAN GRAMMAR OF THE WHOLE APP: "I think X will happen because Y is
occurring, and I'll be wrong if Z happens."
Setup #1 BREAKOUT (first because visually intuitive): resistance → approach →
confirmation → breakout. Setup #2 PULLBACK: trend → pullback to support →
confirmation → continuation. THOSE TWO ONLY. Do NOT dump wedges/flags/
triangles/C&H/H&S/ICT/order blocks/Elliott/Fib on a new trader.
Human video: "Why I pass on most setups" — no trade is a valid decision.
Kai Setup Challenge: 8 charts → Trade / Watch / Pass + explain why. Kai:
"Correct. Trend is bullish, but price is still below resistance. This belongs
on Watch—not Trade." Teaches patience immediately.
Gate: identify breakout, pullback, setup-vs-noise, articulate basic thesis.

## DAY 4 — Build the Trade (~50 min)
L1 Entry: SETUP ≠ ENTRY. Interesting ≠ enter now. Introduce confirmation.
L2 Stop (visual): never "use a 5% stop" — WHERE IS YOUR IDEA WRONG? Stop
goes beyond invalidation.
L3 Target: where could price reasonably move → Risk:Reward.
L4 Position Size, only necessary math: $5,000 acct · 1% max risk = $50 ·
entry $100 stop $98 = $2/share → 25 shares. Kai calculates eventually; they
must understand why.
Interactive Trade Builder: user physically sets 🟢Entry 🔴Stop 🎯Target →
app displays Risk $100 / Reward $300 / R:R 3.0. Kai grades it (e.g. "B+ —
strong entry, target logical; problem: stop inside normal price noise") and
shows his preferred placement.
Gate: build 3 trades — logical entry, logical stop, R:R ≥ threshold, sizing
correct.

## DAY 5 — Execute Without Doing Dumb Shit (~40 min, mostly behavioral)
Orders: market, limit, stop. That's sufficient.
Management: before entry plan the trade; after entry trade the plan.
FOUR MISTAKES, explicitly: 1 Chasing ("did you miss the trade?" yes — don't
chase) · 2 Moving stops (not because you're afraid of losing) · 3 Oversizing
(loss feels catastrophic because position too large) · 4 Revenge trading
(one loss becomes five).
Human video: "The trades I wish I never took" — actual trader scars, not
motivational fluff.
Kai Emotional Intervention sim: trade goes against user. "Your stop is
$182.40. Price is $182.72. What should you do?" Move stop lower / Add more /
Follow the plan / Exit emotionally → correct: follow the predefined plan.

## DAY 6 — Paper Trade With Kai (~60 min) — no more normal lessons
Sim 1 GUIDED: Kai holds hand — trend? resistance (tap)? setup? entry? stop?
target? size? Then simulate subsequent candles.
Sim 2 ASSISTED: fewer questions — build the trade, Kai reviews.
Sim 3 INDEPENDENT: "TSLA. Analyze this chart and submit your trade plan."
Direction, setup, entry, stop, target, risk, reason. Kai grades.
INTRODUCE THE JOURNAL: Before — why did I enter? After — what happened?
Review — DID I FOLLOW MY PLAN? (matters more than did I make money).
Good process > random profits.

## DAY 7 — Live Market Readiness (~60 min)
P1 Final Assessment: ~10 rapid-fire scenarios (trend, support, valid setup?,
invalidation, good stop?, R:R, size, chase or pass).
P2 Full Trade Exam: one chart, no assistance — thesis, symbol, direction,
setup, why now, entry, stop, target, risk, invalidation. Kai scores by
dimension (Analysis 87% · Risk Management 94% · Execution 81% · Overall 87%).
P3 KAI LIVE HANDOFF — the magical moment. Not "course complete":
"You're Trade Ready. Training Mode is changing. From here, I won't teach
every decision before you make it. I'll help you find opportunities,
challenge your reasoning, monitor your setups, and step in when you need me.
[Enter Kai-Assisted Trading →]" → lands on the REAL Home; Kai: "Morning.
You're officially out of Foundations. I've got three setups worth looking at
today. Want to analyze the first one together?"
