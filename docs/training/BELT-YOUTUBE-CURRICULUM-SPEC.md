# Curated YouTube as the human layer of the belt curriculum (owner, 2026-09-07)

DECISION: no separate YouTube library. YouTube IS the human-instruction layer
INSIDE the belt curriculum. Every required lesson = CCAI interactive → curated
YouTube explanation → Kai application → mastery check. User never feels "sent
to YouTube" — CCAI assigned THIS video because it teaches the concept this
belt needs. ~20-30 curated videos replaces recording 20-30 ourselves.

LESSON SHAPE (example Day 2 Market Structure): 1 CCAI Learn (3m interactive
HH/HL/LH/LL) → 2 Watch (curated 6-12m video) → 3 Kai Practice (5 charts) →
4 Apply (mark trend + invalidation on real chart) → 5 Mastery ≥80% → 6 Belt
competency updates.

BELT LADDER (competency groups):
White I: what is a stock · how markets work · candlestick basics
White II: market structure · trend · support/resistance
Yellow I: breakouts · pullbacks · trade vs watch vs pass
Yellow II: entry confirmation · stop placement · risk/reward · position sizing
Blue qualification: trade management · discipline · order types · paper
trading · journaling
Purple/Black: progressively advanced libraries.

CURATION BAR (strict): clear, concise, visually useful, not hypey, no
gambling-pushing, minimal jargon, 5-20 min preferred, concept-specific,
accurate, belt-appropriate. REJECT: course/Discord pitching, "1000% strategy"
nonsense, anything conflicting with CCAI risk philosophy.

REDUNDANCY per competency: Primary video + Backup + Optional deeper dive
(protects against deletion/private/outdated).

KAI NORMALIZES: after every video Kai says "here's how CCAI uses this
concept" (e.g. "the instructor calls this a demand zone; in Foundations we
call the area support — same idea"). External instructors never control the
curriculum; CCAI does.

TIMESTAMPED SEGMENTS: lessons may assign only the useful section ("Watch
4:12–10:35. Focus on how he identifies higher lows; ignore the order-block
discussion for now").

CONTENT OBJECT per belt lesson: lesson · belt · required competency · CCAI
interactive (min) · YouTube (min) · Kai drill (min) · assessment (min) ·
total · pass condition (80%) · skills updated → next lesson unlocks.

XP — VIDEO COMPLETION ≠ MASTERY (anti-farming): interactive +10 XP · video
+10 · Kai practice +20 · assessment passed +30. Assessment + application are
load-bearing; belts cannot be farmed by letting videos play.

THREE LAYERS LONG-TERM: CCAI Core (proprietary curriculum + assessments) ·
Curated Experts (best public educators) · Kai (connective tissue that
teaches, tests, adapts, applies).

NEXT MOVE: master Belt Curriculum Matrix — every competency White I → Black,
best YouTube lesson attached to each = the full content-production blueprint.
