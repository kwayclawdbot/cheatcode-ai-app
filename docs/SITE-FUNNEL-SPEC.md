# cheatcode.com — the website IS the product demo (owner, 2026-09-08)

PRINCIPLE: identify who they are in ~10s, then walk them through the exact
CCAI experience they'd care about. Funnel = Who are you? → Experience CCAI
solving your problem → See your future inside the product → Choose your plan.
NOT headline → feature grid → testimonial → pricing.

FIRST SCREEN (almost empty): "What do you want CCAI to help you do?"
🌱 Learn investing & trading — I'm new or still building confidence.
⚡ Find better swing trades — I already trade and want alerts, analysis & community.
✦ Trade with an AI copilot — I'm experienced and want advanced tools, charting & performance development.

EACH ANSWER → 60–90s INTERACTIVE SIMULATION (clickable, not marketing copy):
- LEARNER: Kai onboarding → 7-Day Path → sample 45s lesson → beginner
  investing pick → practice question → belt progression → Beginner community
  → "Start Learning."
- SWING: today's alerts → open an NVDA alert → entry/stop/target → Ask Kai
  "why this trade?" → community discussion → track the setup → "Get My Alerts."
- PRO: open chart → ask Kai to analyze → Kai marks structure/levels → create
  trade plan → simulated trade graded → Black Belt progression → leaderboard/
  community reputation → "Trade With Kai."

PRICING ONLY AFTER VALUE, PERSONALIZED: Beginner $29 · Intermediate $59 ·
Pro $99. Same CCAI underneath; different sales experience.
(NOTE: $29 Beginner tier does not exist in plans.ts yet — product decision.)

RESPONSIVE SPLIT:
- DESKTOP/large tablet: full brand landing (Learn · Invest · Trade · Alerts ·
  Community · Kai · Progression) + optional "Find your CCAI path" interactive.
- MOBILE: NO mini-desktop scroll. Behaves like the app: experience selector
  immediately → personalized walkthrough → personalized plan offer → Get the
  App / Start Free. Mobile marketing = pre-onboarding funnel.

ANSWER PERSISTS: the selector's answer carries into app install/signup so the
